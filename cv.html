Doctoral research

School of Public Health, The University of Hong Kong

Hong Kong SAR, China

2022-Present

COVID-19 severity pyramid and in-hospital progression

Investigated how changing admission practices altered COVID-19 severity estimates and longitudinal in-hospital progression. Modeled about 2.8 million linked case records, including about 120,000 hospitalizations, using Bayesian severity-pyramid, Aalen-Johansen competing-risk, and Cox models.

Hospital pressure, admission selection, and fatality risk

Assessed how hospital pressure, admission selection, and admission severity shaped the observed in-hospital fatality. Applied period-specific generalized additive models and model-based counterfactual standardization to about 120,000 hospitalizations.

Causal estimands and selection bias in test-negative designs

Examined when vaccine-induced severity attenuation drives test-negative estimates away from source-population causal estimands. Used causal DAGs, analytic bias derivations, Monte Carlo simulation, and inverse-probability weighting to evaluate bias and correction strategies.

Hospital-based test-negative design estimates of vaccine effectiveness (ongoing)

Uses simulation to evaluate when hospital-based TNDs recover vaccine effectiveness against target-pathogen hospitalization, benchmarked against randomized-trial and adjusted-cohort estimates under alternative hospital eligibility, testing, and control-selection mechanisms.
